# AI Bias Workshop – CV Experiment

## Purpose

This exercise demonstrates how **subtle language differences** influence human perception — and how the same mechanism is learned and amplified by AI systems.

The goal is to show that:

- Bias does not require different qualifications
- Language alone can shift evaluation outcomes
- AI trained on biased data will replicate and scale these effects

---

## 📦 Materials

- `CV_John.pdf`
- `CV_Maria.pdf`
- `Scoring_Sheet.pdf`

---

## Experiment Setup

### Step 1 — Introduction

Say:

> "You are now part of a hiring committee.  
> Two candidates. Same role. Limited time."

Distribute:
- Both CVs
- Scoring sheet

---

### Step 2 — Individual Evaluation

Instruction:

> "Please evaluate both candidates independently. Do not discuss yet."

Rate:

- Competence
- Leadership potential
- Communication
- Overall hiring decision

---

### Step 3 — Collect Results


- Who rated John higher in leadership?
- Who rated Maria higher in communication?
- Who would hire John? Who would hire Maria?

---

## Explanation

### John (dominant-coded language)
- led
- drove
- delivered
- optimized

### Maria (support-coded language)
- contributed
- supported
- collaborated
- assisted

---

## 🧠 Key Insight

> Language shapes perception.  
> Perception shapes decisions.  
> Decisions shape reality.
